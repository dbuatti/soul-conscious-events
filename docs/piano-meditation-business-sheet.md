# Piano Meditation — Business Sheet

## Venue & time analysis

**Wellness/music events by state:** VIC 25 · SA 21 · QLD 14 · NSW 10 · TAS 5 · WA 2

**Best venue type — a yoga/wellness studio, not a hall.** Venues already hosting meditation/sound/music events:
- **The Serenity Studio**, **Qi Flow Studio** (SA) — highest-frequency wellness venues
- **Estuary Yoga Space**, **Centre of You**, **Merkaba Studio**, **Breathe Stretch Float**, **Fairfield Wellness** (VIC)

Avoid churches/community halls/beachfront parks — echoey and busy. A studio gives acoustic intimacy, mats/blankets, and an existing wellness audience.

**Best time:**
- Proven wellness time slots: **10–11am** and **5:30–7:30pm**
- **Thursday is the quietest day** (7 events/week vs 44 Saturday, 32 Wednesday) → lowest competition
- Saturday/Sunday = biggest foot traffic but most competition

**Pick:** Sunday early evening (~6–7pm) for reach, or Thursday evening to stand out. Skip Wednesday.

## Title

Golden Hour Piano — *A Guided Hour of Improvised Piano & Stillness*

## What it is

A 60-minute, candlelit seated meditation where a single pianist improvises continuously (no set list, no talking) while participants lie or sit on mats and let the music guide them. Ambient-only — no headphones, no facilitator. Starts with a 3-min guided breath, ends with 5-min silence.

## Bio (for the listing)

> An hour of pure, unbroken piano, played live in the moment. No sheet music, no agenda — just sound moving through the room while you rest, breathe, and let your nervous system downshift. Bring a mat and blanket; leave everything else at the door.

## Pros

- Minimal setup — one musician, one room, mats, low lighting
- Very low running cost (no band, no production)
- Pairs naturally with existing wellness audience (meditation/sound-bath crowd)
- Highly repeatable → easy recurring weekly slot
- Strong "premium calm" positioning that photographs well for IG

## Cons

- Single point of failure — one pianist, no redundancy
- Audience must already be into stillness (not a casual walk-in event)
- Smaller per-session ticket revenue vs. a workshop/retreat (recommend $25–$35)
- Depends on venue having a good piano or a quality digital + PA
- Quiet, niche category — needs strong marketing to fill the room

## Suggested pricing

$25–$35 (DB shows $25 and free/donation are the most common wellness price points; paid wellness events run $25–$50)
